# MIT ADT University, Pune: Admissions Questions and Answers

> **SAMPLE DOCUMENT FOR TESTING.** This was written to demo an admissions answering agent. The structure follows a typical
> Indian private university, but every fee, date, seat count, cut-off and phone number below is an illustrative example,
> not an official figure. Check the university's own admissions office and website before relying on any of it.

## 1. About the university

**Q: What is MIT ADT University?**
A: MIT ADT University is a private university in Loni Kalbhor, Pune, Maharashtra, run by the MIT Group of Institutions. It
offers undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programmes across engineering, design, management, film and television,
liberal arts, and other schools.

**Q: Where is the campus?**
A: The main campus is at Rajbaug, Loni Kalbhor, Pune, Maharashtra 412201, about 20 km from Pune city centre on the
Pune-Solapur highway.

**Q: Is the university recognised?**
A: The university is established under a Maharashtra state Act and is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC).
Engineering programmes seek AICTE approval and several programmes are accredited by NAAC. (Sample: confirm current grades.)

**Q: What are the main schools?**
A: Engineering and Technology, Design, Management, Film and Television, Architecture, Computer Science and AI, Liberal Arts,
Sciences, and Law (sample list).

## 2. Programmes offered

**Q: Which undergraduate programmes can I join?**
A: B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering, Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, Mechanical, Civil, Electronics and
Telecommunication, and Electrical; B.Des in Product, Communication, and Fashion Design; BBA; B.Arch; BA in Film Making; BCA;
B.Sc in Data Science.

**Q: Which postgraduate programmes are there?**
A: M.Tech (Computer Science, Data Science, Structural, VLSI), MBA (Marketing, Finance, Business Analytics, HR), M.Des, MCA,
M.Sc, and MA programmes.

**Q: How long is each programme?**
A: B.Tech and B.Des: four years. B.Arch: five years. BBA, BCA, B.Sc: three years. M.Tech, MBA, M.Des, MCA: two years.

**Q: Can I switch branch after the first year?**
A: Yes, in B.Tech, a student with a CGPA of at least 8.0 after the first year can apply for a branch change, subject to
vacant seats in the requested branch. Applications open in June.

## 3. Eligibility

**Q: What is the eligibility for B.Tech?**
A: Passed class 12 (10+2) with Physics and Mathematics plus Chemistry, Biology, Computer Science or Technical Vocational
subjects, with at least 50% marks (45% for reserved categories) in these subjects together.

**Q: What entrance exam do I need for B.Tech?**
A: Any of MHT-CET, JEE Main, or the university's own MIT-ADT CET score is accepted. A valid score from any one is enough.

**Q: Is there an entrance test for design courses?**
A: Yes. B.Des and M.Des applicants take the university's Design Aptitude Test (DAT), then a studio round and portfolio review.

**Q: What is the eligibility for BBA and MBA?**
A: BBA: class 12 in any stream with 50% marks. MBA: a bachelor's degree with 50% marks and a valid score in CAT, MAT, CMAT or
the university's MBA entrance test.

**Q: Can I apply with a diploma?**
A: Yes. A three-year diploma holder can join B.Tech in the second year through the lateral entry route, with at least 50%
marks in the diploma.

**Q: Is there an age limit?**
A: There is no upper age limit for most programmes. The candidate must be at least 17 years old on 31 December of the
admission year.

## 4. Application process

**Q: How do I apply?**
A: Apply online on the university admissions portal: register with a mobile number and email, fill in the application form,
upload documents, pay the application fee, and submit. You will get an application number by SMS and email.

**Q: What is the application fee?**
A: Rs. 1,000 for most programmes and Rs. 1,500 for design programmes (sample figures). The fee is non-refundable.

**Q: What are the key dates this year?**
A: Applications open on 1 December and close on 30 June (sample). Entrance tests: first week of May for CET and DAT.
First merit list: 10 June. Second merit list: 25 June. Last date to confirm admission: 10 July. Classes begin: 1 August.

**Q: Can I apply after the last date?**
A: Applications are accepted until seats are filled, with a late fee of Rs. 500. Seats in popular programmes fill early.

**Q: How do I check my application status?**
A: Log in to the admissions portal with your application number and registered mobile number. Status shows as Submitted,
Under Review, Shortlisted, Offered, Accepted or Rejected.

## 5. Documents required

**Q: Which documents do I need?**
A: Class 10 and 12 marksheets, entrance exam scorecard, passport photos, Aadhaar card, transfer or leaving certificate,
migration certificate for students from other boards, caste certificate and validity (for reserved categories),
domicile certificate for state quota seats, and a gap certificate if there is a break of a year or more.

**Q: Do I upload documents or submit them in person?**
A: You upload scans while applying and bring the originals for verification on the reporting day.

**Q: Is a character certificate needed?**
A: Yes, from the last institution attended, submitted at the time of joining.

## 6. Fees and payments

**Q: What are the annual tuition fees?** (sample figures, per year)
A: B.Tech: Rs. 2,40,000. B.Des: Rs. 2,80,000. BBA: Rs. 1,60,000. MBA: Rs. 3,20,000. B.Arch: Rs. 2,50,000. MCA: Rs. 1,50,000.

**Q: Are there other charges besides tuition?**
A: Yes. A one-time admission and registration fee of Rs. 25,000, a refundable caution deposit of Rs. 10,000, an examination
fee of Rs. 5,000 per year, and a development fee of Rs. 15,000 per year.

**Q: What are the hostel fees?**
A: Rs. 95,000 per year for a double-sharing room and Rs. 1,25,000 for a single room, including electricity and Wi-Fi.
Mess charges are Rs. 60,000 per year for vegetarian and non-vegetarian meals.

**Q: Can I pay in instalments?**
A: Yes. Tuition can be paid in two equal instalments: one at admission and one by 1 January. Education loans are accepted
and the admissions office provides a bonafide letter for the bank.

**Q: Which payment methods are accepted?**
A: UPI, net banking, debit and credit cards on the portal, and demand draft payable to "MIT ADT University, Pune".

**Q: What is the refund policy?**
A: If you withdraw before 15 July, the fee is refunded less Rs. 10,000. From 16 July to 31 July, 50% of tuition is refunded.
After 1 August, no tuition is refunded, but the caution deposit is returned at the end of the course.

## 7. Scholarships and financial aid

**Q: What scholarships are available?**
A: Merit scholarships: 100% tuition waiver for the top 5 CET or JEE scorers, 50% for the next 20, 25% for the next 50.
Sports scholarships up to 50%. A sibling discount of 10% when a brother or sister is also enrolled.

**Q: Is there a government scholarship?**
A: Students eligible under the Maharashtra government schemes (EBC, OBC, SC/ST, minority) can claim scholarships through the
MahaDBT portal. The admissions office helps with the forms.

**Q: How do I apply for a merit scholarship?**
A: It is considered automatically when you apply. The scholarship letter comes with your offer.

**Q: Can scholarships be lost?**
A: Yes. Merit scholarships continue only if you keep a CGPA of at least 8.0 and 75% attendance each year.

## 8. Seats, reservation and cut-offs

**Q: How many seats are there?**
A: B.Tech: 1,200 seats in total across branches (Computer Science 240, AI and Data Science 120, Mechanical 120, Civil 60,
others 60 each; sample figures). BBA: 180. MBA: 240. B.Des: 120.

**Q: Is there reservation?**
A: Yes. Seats are reserved as per the Maharashtra government rules: SC 13%, ST 7%, VJ/NT and OBC as notified, EWS 10%, and
5% for persons with disabilities. Fifteen percent of seats are for NRI and international students.

**Q: What was last year's cut-off for Computer Science?**
A: Last year's general category closing score was about 92 percentile in MHT-CET or 85 percentile in JEE Main (sample).

**Q: What are the chances with a lower score?**
A: Programmes with unfilled seats open a spot round after the second merit list. Applicants with 60 to 80 percentile can
still apply for Mechanical, Civil and Electrical.

## 9. Campus, hostel and facilities

**Q: Is hostel accommodation available?**
A: Yes, separate hostels for boys and girls on campus. First-year students get priority. Apply through the portal after
receiving your offer.

**Q: What facilities does the campus have?**
A: Central library with 1.5 lakh volumes, innovation labs, a design studio, a film studio, sports complex with cricket
ground and indoor courts, gym, medical centre with an ambulance, canteens, banks and ATMs, and Wi-Fi throughout.

**Q: Is there transport?**
A: University buses run from Pune Station, Hadapsar, Katraj, Swargate, and Pimpri Chinchwad. Annual fee: Rs. 30,000.

**Q: Are there rules for hostel timings?**
A: Hostel gates close at 10 p.m. for first-year students and 11 p.m. for others. Visitors are allowed from 4 to 6 p.m. on
Sundays with a registered visitor pass.

## 10. Academics and placements

**Q: Which language is used for teaching?**
A: English. Faculty also explain in Hindi and Marathi when students need it.

**Q: What is the grading system?**
A: Choice-based credit system with a ten-point CGPA. Minimum passing marks are 40% in each subject.

**Q: What is the attendance requirement?**
A: At least 75% attendance in each subject to sit for the end-semester exam.

**Q: How are the placements?**
A: Last year 85% of eligible students were placed (sample). The highest package was Rs. 32 lakh per annum and the average
Rs. 6.5 lakh. Recruiters include IT services companies, product companies, banks, and design studios.

**Q: Does the university offer internships?**
A: Yes. A summer internship is compulsory after the second year, and a six-month industry internship is offered in the final
year.

**Q: Are there foreign exchange programmes?**
A: Yes, semester-exchange partnerships with selected universities in Europe and Asia are available for students with a
CGPA of 8.5 or higher (sample).

## 11. International and NRI students

**Q: Can international students apply?**
A: Yes. They need an equivalent class 12 certificate (verified by the Association of Indian Universities), a valid passport
and a student visa, and proof of English proficiency. Tuition for international students is in US dollars.

**Q: What are the fees for NRI students?**
A: B.Tech tuition for NRI and international students is USD 6,000 per year (sample), plus hostel and other charges.

## 12. Contact and support

**Q: How do I contact the admissions office?**
A: Phone: +91 20 0000 0000 (sample number). Email: admissions@example.edu (sample address). Office hours: Monday to
Saturday, 9:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m.

**Q: Can I visit the campus?**
A: Yes. Campus tours run on weekdays at 11 a.m. and 3 p.m. Book through the website or call the office.

**Q: Who do I speak to about a complaint or grievance?**
A: Write to the Student Grievance Cell. Complaints are answered within seven working days.

**Q: What should I do if the portal does not work?**
A: Clear your browser data and retry, or email a screenshot of the error with your application number to the helpdesk.
