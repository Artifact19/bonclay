# Lakeview Public Library - Member Handbook

Sample data for a fictional organisation.

## About the library
Lakeview Public Library is a community library at 22 Garden Road, Kothrud, Pune 411038. The front desk phone number is 020-5550-0177 and the email is help@lakeviewlibrary.example. The library is closed on public holidays and on the second Monday of every month for stock-taking.

## Opening hours
- Monday to Friday: 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM.
- Saturday: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
- Sunday: 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM, reading room only. Books cannot be issued or returned on Sunday.
- During exam weeks in March and October the reading room stays open until 10:00 PM, Monday to Saturday.

## Membership and fees
- Student membership: Rs. 300 per year. A valid student ID card is needed.
- Adult membership: Rs. 600 per year.
- Senior citizen membership (60 years and above): Rs. 300 per year.
- Family membership (up to four people at one address): Rs. 1,000 per year.
- Refundable security deposit: Rs. 500 for every membership type. It is returned when the membership is closed and all books are back.
- Membership cards are issued on the same day when the form is submitted with one photo and an address proof.

## Borrowing rules
- Student and adult members can borrow up to 3 books at a time. Family and senior members can borrow up to 4.
- The loan period is 14 days. A book can be renewed twice, for 7 days each time, unless another member has reserved it.
- Reference books, newspapers and the latest issues of magazines cannot be taken out of the library.
- Up to 2 DVDs can be borrowed for 7 days, and they cannot be renewed.

## Late fees and lost books
- The late fee is Rs. 2 per day per book. The fee for one book is capped at Rs. 100.
- A member with unpaid late fees above Rs. 50 cannot borrow new books until the fees are paid.
- A lost book must be replaced with the same edition or paid for at the current price plus a Rs. 50 handling charge.
- A damaged book is charged according to the damage, up to the full price of the book.

## Reading room, computers and Wi-Fi
- The reading room has 60 seats. Silence must be kept; phones are kept on silent.
- Eight computers are available for members, one hour per day each. Slots can be booked at the front desk.
- Free Wi-Fi is available to members. The password is given at the front desk and changes every month.
- Printing costs Rs. 2 per page in black and white and Rs. 10 per page in colour.

## Events and children's section
- The children's story hour runs every Saturday from 11:00 AM to 12:00 noon, for ages 4 to 10. Entry is free for members.
- A book club for adults meets on the first Sunday of each month at 11:00 AM.
- Visitors who are not members can use the reading room for Rs. 20 per day.

## Donations and suggestions
- The library accepts donated books in good condition on weekdays between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM. Textbooks older than five years and torn books are not accepted.
- Members can suggest new books by filling the suggestion form at the front desk or emailing help@lakeviewlibrary.example. Purchases are decided once a month.

## What the library cannot do
The library cannot lend books to non-members, cannot accept payment by cheque, and does not offer a home delivery service.
