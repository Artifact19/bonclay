# Gyan Jyoti School - School Fee Reminder Guide

Sample data for a fictional organisation.

## About Gyan Jyoti School
Gyan Jyoti School is a fictional school in Pune. Office hours: Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM. The school office is closed on Sunday. Office phone: 020-XXXX-1234.

## Sample fee used in this demo
- Fee: Term 2 tuition fee, per student.
- Classes 1 to 5: ₹18,500 per student.
- Classes 6 to 10: ₹22,000 per student.
- Due date: 10 November 2026.

## How to pay
1. UPI: pay to the UPI ID gyanjyoti.fees@examplebank from any UPI app, and write the child's name and class in the remarks.
2. School office: pay in cash or by card at the school office during office hours (Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM).
3. Bank transfer: account details are printed only on the official fee receipt. The school never shares bank account details by phone.

## Late fee
- A late fee of ₹50 per day applies for every day the fee is paid after 10 November 2026.
- The late fee is capped at ₹1,000.

## Concessions
- Sibling concession: 10 percent off the tuition fee of the second child.
- Merit and need-based concessions: apply by written application to the principal before 31 October 2026. Phone requests are not accepted for these.

## Security - very important
Gyan Jyoti School NEVER asks for OTP, UPI PIN, ATM PIN, password or card CVV on a call. The agent must never ask for these, and must never accept them if a caller offers them. The agent must tell the parent not to share these with anyone. Parents who receive such a call should report it to the school office at 020-XXXX-1234.

## Refunds and fee changes
Refunds and fee changes are handled only through the school office, in person or in writing. They are not handled by phone.

## Things the agent cannot do
The agent cannot waive fees, change the due date, approve a concession, or process a refund. For these, the parent should contact the school office at 020-XXXX-1234 during office hours, or visit the office.
