# Kisan Mitra - Crop and Scheme Helpline Guide

Sample data for a fictional organisation.

## About Kisan Mitra
Kisan Mitra is a fictional farmer helpline run for farmers in Maharashtra and nearby states. Helpline number: 1800-555-0123 (toll free). Helpline hours: every day from 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM, except Sunday afternoons (closed after 1:00 PM).

## Sowing calendar basics (general guide)
- Kharif season (sowing June to July, harvest October to November): soybean, cotton, tur (pigeon pea), jowar, bajra, maize, rice. Sow after the first good monsoon rain, when the soil has about 75 to 100 mm of moisture.
- Rabi season (sowing October to December, harvest February to April): wheat, gram (chana), rabi jowar, onion, safflower. Wheat is best sown between 1 November and 25 November.
- Summer season (sowing February to March): groundnut, moong, vegetables, and fodder crops, which need assured irrigation.
- Seed rate for wheat: about 100 kg per hectare. Seed rate for soybean: about 65 kg per hectare. Treat seed before sowing as advised by the local agriculture officer.

## Soil testing
- Test the soil once every 2 to 3 years, before the season starts.
- Take samples from 8 to 10 spots in the field at 6 inches depth, mix, and give about 500 grams in a clean cloth bag.
- Submit it at the nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) or taluka agriculture office. The test fee is ₹30 per sample and the report comes in 10 to 15 days with fertiliser advice.

## Sample Input Subsidy Scheme (Beej Anudan Yojana, fictional)
- What it gives: 50 percent subsidy on certified seed, up to ₹5,000 per farmer per season.
- Who can apply: farmers with up to 5 hectares of land and a land record (7/12 extract) in their own name.
- Documents: 7/12 extract, Aadhaar card, bank passbook copy, and a passport photo.
- How to apply: 1) fill the form at the Gram Panchayat or taluka agriculture office, 2) attach the documents, 3) get an acknowledgement number, 4) the subsidy is paid into the bank account within 30 days after approval.
- Last date to apply for the Kharif season: 15 June. For the Rabi season: 15 October.

## Weather and pests
- Do not spray pesticides before expected rain or in strong wind.
- For pest or disease problems, take a leaf sample to the nearest KVK instead of guessing the medicine.

## Important
This helpline gives general information only. Farmers should always check the final advice, dosage and scheme rules with their local agriculture officer before acting. The helpline cannot approve subsidies or promise payment dates.
