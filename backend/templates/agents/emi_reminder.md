# Shubh Finance - EMI Reminder Guide

Sample data for a fictional organisation.

## About Shubh Finance
Shubh Finance is a fictional non-banking finance company that gives two-wheeler and personal loans. Customer care number: 1800-555-0191 (toll free), open Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM. Registered office: 7 Residency Road, Nashik 422001.

## Sample loan used in this demo
- Customer: Mr. Sanjay Pawar. Loan type: two-wheeler loan, account ending 4821.
- Monthly EMI: ₹3,450.
- Due date: the 5th of every month. The next EMI is due on 5 November 2026.
- Remaining instalments: 14.
- A grace period of 3 days applies. After 8 November, the payment is treated as overdue.

## Explaining the EMI
The EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment) is a fixed amount paid every month. It covers part of the principal and the interest. Paying on time keeps the credit score healthy.

## How to pay
1. UPI: pay to the UPI ID shubhfinance@upi from any UPI app, and write the loan account number (last 4 digits: 4821) in the remarks.
2. Branch: visit any Shubh Finance branch between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM, Monday to Friday. Cash, cheque and demand draft are accepted at the branch.
3. Auto-debit (NACH): the customer can register a bank mandate at any branch so the EMI is deducted automatically on the 5th.
- A payment receipt is sent by SMS within 2 hours of a successful payment.

## Late payment
- Late fee: ₹500 plus 2 percent per month on the overdue EMI amount, if the EMI is not paid by 8th of the month.
- If the EMI stays unpaid for 60 days, the account is reported to the credit bureau.
- Customers who face difficulty can ask for a short extension or a restructure plan by calling customer care before the due date. No promise is made on the phone.

## Security - very important
Shubh Finance NEVER asks for OTP, UPI PIN, ATM PIN, card number, CVV, net-banking password or any other password, on a call, SMS or WhatsApp. The agent must never ask for these. If a caller offers them, the agent must stop them and say not to share these with anyone. Payments are made only through the UPI ID or branch listed above.

## Things the agent cannot do
The agent cannot waive late fees, change the due date, or close a loan. For these, the customer should call 1800-555-0191 or visit a branch.
