# Office of the Head of Department, Artificial Intelligence

Sample data for a fictional organisation. These are placeholder rules for the "HOD Office Assistant" template: replace them with
the department's real ones, because this document is the only source of facts the agent uses for questions, apart from the
meeting tools.

## Who this office is for

Prof. Jayashri Prasad is the Head of the Department of Artificial Intelligence. This office handles meetings with her and
common department questions for students, parents and faculty.

## Meeting the HOD

- Meetings are 20 minutes long, in the HOD cabin of the AI Department.
- The HOD sees visitors on Monday to Friday, from 10:30 am to 1:00 pm and from 2:00 pm to 4:30 pm. There are no meetings
  on Saturdays and Sundays, and none from 11:30 am to 12:00 noon, which is the department meeting.
- Meetings can be requested up to 30 days ahead and at least one hour before the slot.
- Every meeting is a **request**. The HOD approves or declines each one. Nothing is confirmed until she approves it, and
  the caller can ask about the request with its number or their phone number.
- The office gives at most 10 meetings a day, and one person can have at most 2 open requests.
- A student gives their name, class and division, and the reason. A parent gives their name, the child's name and class,
  and the reason. Faculty and visitors give their name and the reason.
- Please carry the college ID card, and any papers about the matter, to the meeting.

## What the HOD does not discuss over the phone

Marks and re-evaluation, individual grievances, admission decisions and personal or medical matters are handled in writing.
Please submit a written application to the department office, addressed to the HOD.

## Common department questions

- **Attendance:** a student needs at least 75 percent attendance in each subject to appear for the end-semester
  examination. Shortage is reviewed by the HOD on a written application with a valid reason.
- **Project guide allotment:** project groups are formed in the first month of the semester. A student who wants to change
  a guide must meet the HOD with a written request signed by the current guide.
- **Department notices:** notices are put on the department notice board and in the class groups. The office does not
  give notice contents over the phone unless the caller asks about a specific notice.
- **Faculty leave:** faculty apply on the leave form to the HOD at least two days before, except in an emergency.
